Privacy policy

Updated November 5, 2026. Describes version 1.0 (17). Developer: Yongbo Xia. Contact: yongboxia@icloud.com.

Camera images stay on your device

The app uses the front camera to display the player and recognize body jumps on the device. Images and body observations are used only for the live game and are not saved or uploaded to a developer server. This version has no video recording and does not use Photos or microphone access.

No account, advertising or tracking

All five worlds are free. There are no purchases, subscriptions, ads, third-party analytics or tracking tools. Playing does not require registration, a name, location, contacts or payment information.

Progress stored locally

Today’s stars, the all-time total, screen orientation, practice style, sound, celebrations and sensitivity preferences are stored on the device. They are not synced to a developer server. Deleting the app removes data held within the app.

Feedback a parent chooses to send

A parent may open system Mail from the app, review, change or remove the message and diagnostic summary, and decide whether to send it. The summary includes the app name and version, device model, iOS version, language, time, practice and camera state, and a temporary feedback reference. It does not attach device identifiers, photos, videos, raw body observations or Apple Account details. Your chosen email service handles the message and sender address. Messages go to yongboxia@icloud.com to help understand and respond to the request, not for advertising. If Mail is not configured, copying the content does not send it automatically. Please do not provide private images of children or your home.

Screen Mirroring and external pages

If a parent enables iOS Screen Mirroring, the system sends the display to the compatible receiver chosen by the user. This does not send it to the developer. Parent confirmation protects the app’s privacy-policy, system Settings and feedback paths. External browsers and services process necessary connection information under their own policies.

Children and grown-ups

A grown-up should help install the app, grant camera access and connect a TV. The app has no social, chat or content-upload feature. You may contact the email address above with privacy questions or ask to delete feedback information you chose to provide.

Website and policy updates

This support website currently serves static pages through GitHub Pages, with no added ads or visit analytics. Hosting services may process necessary connection information under their policies. We update the date on this page when features or data handling change.
