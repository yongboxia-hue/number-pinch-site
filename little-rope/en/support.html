A little help for your next jump

How do we start?

Place your iPhone securely. Choose portrait or landscape on the home screen, tap Let’s go and allow camera access. Choose No rope or With rope on the world-selection page, then tap a world. Keep the head and feet in view and stand still for the countdown. A grown-up should stay nearby, with clear space to move.

Can we play with or without a rope?

Yes. Choose your style above the worlds; the app remembers it for next time. Both styles count body jumps, not rope clearance or exercise quality. With a rope, leave plenty of room to swing it.

What if the camera cannot see the whole body?

This version uses the front camera only. Adjust the phone’s height, angle and your position, leaving space above the head and below the feet. Use good lighting and keep the feet visible. Stand still while the camera finds you again.

Why are some jumps missed?

Keep the phone steady and your whole body visible. Try Settings > Practice options to adjust jump sensitivity. Briefly leaving the picture does not remove collected stars; stand still again when you return. If the count differs from what you observe, email the steps and readings. You do not need to send pictures of your child.

How do we finish Free Sky?

Tap Pause, then Finish and keep my stars. All five worlds are free, with no purchases or subscriptions. Free Sky has no jump limit.

Can we play on a TV?

Open Settings > Connect a TV and follow the guide to system Screen Mirroring. Your iPhone and receiver need a compatible mirroring method and the same home network. Return to the game after connecting. You can keep playing on the phone if a TV is unavailable.

Camera access and local progress

Camera tracking stays on the device. Images are not saved or uploaded. This version has no video recording and needs no Photos or microphone access. If camera access was denied, a parent can enable it in system Settings. Stars and preferences stay locally; deleting the app removes its app data.

Contact support

Open Settings > Feedback and ask a parent to complete confirmation. The system Mail interface lets the parent review or remove the diagnostic summary and choose whether to send the email. If no Mail account is configured, copy the content into your usual email app. You can also contact yongboxia@icloud.com. Please do not send private images of children or your home.
